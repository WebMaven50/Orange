The Famine Years

Genesis 41. Pharaoh dreams of seven fat cows swallowed by seven lean ones. Joseph reads the sign: seven years of plenty, then seven of famine that will consume the land. You are steward of your own household. Prepare wisely, or watch it starve.

Fourteen years pass, one decision at a time. In the years of plenty, you choose how much of the harvest to store against what's coming. In the years of famine, the choice is no longer yours to make so easily — only how you survive it.

Years 1–7 — Plenty. Store, balance, or feast on each harvest.
Years 8–14 — Famine. Reserves are drawn down. Shocks happen. Choices matter.
Run dry at any point, and the reckoning ends early.
