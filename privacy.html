Privacy Policy

Orange Robot (game) and Orange Robot Lab  ·  Last updated: October 2026

In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your robots and notes stay on your phone. The only things that leave your device are optional anonymous counters and, if you choose to post a score, the initials you type.

What we do not collect

Your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. The apps contain no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

What stays on your device

Your robots and rules, test reports, notes, live tests and settings are stored on your phone only. We never receive them.

Anonymous usage counts

The apps can send a simple count when something happens, such as “the prologue was played” or “the book link was tapped”. A count contains no identifier and no personal information. On our server we keep only how many times each event happened per day. You can turn this off in the app (Info screen in the game, Settings in the Lab), after which the app sends nothing.

Leaderboard (Orange Robot game)

If you choose to post a score, the initials you type (up to three characters), your final score and your virtual sats are stored on our server and shown publicly on the leaderboard. Posting is optional. Please don't use your real name or anything personal as your initials.

Prices and the internet

Orange Robot Lab downloads public daily Bitcoin prices from Kraken and Coinbase. Those requests contain no personal information. The game's leaderboard and usage counts are handled by our own servers on Cloudflare.

Notifications (Orange Robot game)

Notifications are off unless you turn them on. If you turn on game messages or Bitcoin price alerts, the game sends our server a random push token for your device, along with your choices: messages on or off, alerts on or off, and the alert percentage.

  • The token is not linked to your name or to any account.
  • We send messages rarely, and only to people who opted in. They may include news, a podcast or a deal.
  • Price alerts come from public Bitcoin prices, not from anything about you.
  • Turning both switches off deletes your token from our server.

Notifications (Orange Robot Lab)

If you switch alerts on, notifications are created on your device to tell you when a paper-trading robot made a hypothetical trade. They are not sent from a server and you can turn them off at any time.

This website (theorangerobot.com)

The website counts visits to understand what people find useful. For each page view or click we record the page, the website the visitor came from (or "direct"), the visitor's approximate location (country, region and city, as estimated by our hosting provider), the type of device (phone, tablet or computer) with its operating system and browser as broad categories only, and what was clicked. We do not keep your full browser details.

  • We do not store IP addresses. Each visit gets a one-way code that changes every day, which lets us count unique visitors but cannot be reversed or followed from one day to the next.
  • These counts are kept for up to 90 days.
  • The site does not use cookies for this. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the site sends nothing.
  • Some pages also use Google Analytics, which has its own privacy policy.

Links

Links in the apps open theorangerobot.com in your browser. Our website has its own pages and is separate from the apps.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Not financial advice

The apps are games and educational simulations. Prices are historical or public market data, all money is virtual, and virtual sats have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or Bitcoin. Nothing in the apps is financial advice and past results do not predict the future.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
